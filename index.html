About

I'm a DevOps Team Lead specialising in AWS, Kubernetes and Terraform, building the infrastructure that lets engineering teams ship faster and operate with confidence. I focus on turning manual, fragile processes into automated, reliable systems: from provisioning cloud resources and managing container platforms, to wiring up full observability stacks and securing CI/CD pipelines end-to-end.

At X-Tention I'm the DevOps Team Lead for MACH, our healthcare integration platform, responsible for its architecture and technical planning. We run it in the cloud on AWS EKS and deliver it on-premises into customer environments. In practice that means GitOps delivery with Helm and Argo CD, GitLab CI/CD pipelines that build, test and publish our releases, and production observability with Prometheus, Thanos, OpenSearch and the Grafana stack (Loki, Tempo, Alloy).

Outside work I've published 108 posts, including a 90-day DevOps series, at blog.estebanmoreno.link, and have earned certifications across AWS, Linux, Terraform and networking. Continuous learning isn't a line on my CV — it's how I work.
